# One-Year Content Strategy

A 52-week sequenced curriculum. Every post assumes only what came before it. A follower who
starts at Week 1 and stays to Week 52 has effectively taken a structured BIM course.

**Positioning:** Vollmann Olamide Akarakiri — BIM specialist, Autodesk Revit professional,
architectural designer. Teaching BIM from first principles, from practice, in a market without
a national mandate.

**Goal:** authority → inbound freelance and consulting → training and course opportunities.

---

## The weekly rhythm

Three posts per week. 156 posts per year. Consistent slots train the audience.

| Day | Slot | Format | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Monday** | **The Lesson** | Text post or carousel | The week's core teaching topic. This is the curriculum. |
| **Wednesday** | **The Practical** | Quick tip, tutorial extract, or myth-vs-fact | Immediately usable. This is what gets saved and shared. |
| **Friday** | **The Professional** | Career, industry observation, or case study | This is what gets you hired. |

`[Rule]` Monday is never skipped. If a week must be cut, cut Wednesday. The Monday sequence is
the course, and a course with gaps stops being a course.

## What to measure

`[OPINION]` Vanity metrics to ignore: impressions, follower count in isolation.

Metrics that indicate the strategy is working:

| Signal | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Saves and shares** | The content is useful, not just agreeable |
| **Comments asking follow-up questions** | You are being read as a teacher |
| **Profile views after posting** | People are checking who you are — the pre-enquiry behaviour |
| **DMs asking about work** | The actual goal |
| **Repeat commenters across weeks** | You are building an audience, not catching a wave |

Review quarterly. If Q1 produces no DMs, the problem is usually the Friday slot — the
professional content is not making it clear what you do or that you are available.
