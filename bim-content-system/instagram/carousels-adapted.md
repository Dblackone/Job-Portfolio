# Instagram Carousel Adaptations

Every LinkedIn carousel adapted for Instagram. Same canvas (1080 × 1350), same brand system,
different content density.

---

## The adaptation rules

| | LinkedIn | Instagram |
|---|---|---|
| **Slides** | 8–12 | 6–8 |
| **Words per slide** | 35–45 | 15–25 |
| **Reading posture** | Considered, desk, professional context | Fast, thumb, mixed context |
| **What carries the meaning** | Text | Visual, with text confirming it |
| **Tone** | Authoritative | Aspirational but credible |
| **Hashtags** | 5–8 at the end | 15–25 after two line breaks |
| **Export** | PDF document post | PNG at 2× |

**The four moves that adapt any carousel:**

1. **Cut to the spine.** Keep hook, the 3–5 load-bearing ideas, the takeaway, the CTA. Drop
   everything that qualifies, nuances, or footnotes.
2. **Promote the visual.** Whatever was supporting the text becomes the slide. Text becomes the
   caption on the slide, not the content of it.
3. **Halve every sentence.** If a sentence has a comma, it is probably two slides or one shorter
   sentence.
4. **Move the nuance to the caption.** Instagram captions are read by the people who care.
   Contested detail, sources, and qualifications live there.

**What never changes:** no emoji · Bebas headlines ALL-CAPS · Inter body sentence case · dark
slide 1 and dark final slide · clay rule on every content slide · footer lockup · no uncited
statistics.

## Instagram-native formats

Not everything should be a carousel. Three formats that work better natively:

### Single-image myth cards

1080 × 1350. Dark scheme. One myth, one correction, nothing else.

```
EYEBROW:  MYTH VS FACT

MYTH
"BIM means 3D."

──── clay rule

FACT
BIM means managed information.
ISO 19650 is a standard about
information management.

Vollmann Akarakiri · BIM & Revit
```

**Bank of 12 ready to build:**

| Myth | Fact |
|---|---|
| BIM means 3D | BIM means managed information |
| Revit is BIM | Revit is a BIM authoring tool |
| A shared folder is a CDE | A CDE has states, approvals, and an audit trail |
| Higher LOD is better | The right level is the one the decision needs |
| The model gives you the BOQ | The model gives you quantities |
| BIM is only for big projects | Small projects have less margin to lose |
| A nice render is a digital twin | A twin receives live data from the real asset |
| 7D BIM means sustainability | The industry does not agree what 7D means |
| BIM Level 2 is the current standard | ISO 19650 does not use that notation |
| EIR means Employer's Information Requirements | In ISO 19650 it means Exchange |
| COBie is a spreadsheet | COBie is a specification; the spreadsheet is a carrier |
| Warnings do not matter | Duplicate instances silently double your quantities |

### Annotated screenshot posts

Single image, real Revit capture, clay callout numbers in 40 px circles, short caption per
number below the image. Highest-saving format in this system.

**Rules:** state the Revit version · crop tight · remove client information · light interface theme.

### Reels covers

When a TikTok script is cross-posted as a Reel, the cover frame uses the dark scheme with the
hook as a Bebas headline. Consistent covers make a grid read as a body of work rather than a
feed.
