# Brand and Design System — Personal BIM Brand

Design instructions for every image and carousel, written to be executed directly in **Canva or
Figma** without further decisions.

---

## Relationship to the DOVA Futures brand

This is a **personal professional brand**, distinct from the DOVA Futures corporate content hub
(`/content-hub`). They are not the same voice: DOVA Futures speaks as a construction company to
clients; this brand speaks as a practitioner to peers and learners.

**Decision:** the personal brand **inherits the DOVA visual language** — the same typefaces and
the same core palette — because Vollmann is DOVA's CEO and the two feeds will be seen by
overlapping audiences. Visual consistency reads as one coherent professional identity; a
completely separate palette would read as two disconnected people.

**Rules inherited from the DOVA Content Kit (§11), applied here in full:**

| Rule | Applied here |
|---|---|
| **No emoji** | Zero emoji in captions, graphics, CTAs, or templates |
| **Casing** | Headlines Bebas Neue ALL-CAPS · eyebrows UPPERCASE Inter · body sentence case |
| **Real metrics only** | No invented statistics. Extended here: no uncited BIM ROI claims of any kind |
| **Colour discipline** | Green primary, cream surface, clay accent capped at 10%. Never purple, blue, or pink |
| **Typography** | Bebas Neue for display, Inter for body/UI. No substitutes |
| **Hook first** | Never open a caption with "I am" or "As a BIM professional" |
| **CTA required** | Every caption ends with a call to action |
| **Hashtag placement** | After two line breaks, never mid-copy |
| **Quality over volume** | One exceptional post beats three mediocre ones |

**Rules that differ, and why:**

| DOVA rule | Personal brand variation | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Currency always ₦ | Currency rarely used; when used, ₦ for local, otherwise state the currency explicitly | Audience is international |
| Real DOVA project photos only | Real project photos **or** Revit/Navisworks screen captures | Teaching content needs software screenshots. Still no stock photography and no AI-generated photography. |
| DOVA logo lockup | Personal name lockup: **VOLLMANN AKARAKIRI** with role line | Personal brand, not corporate |
| Voice: premium, corporate | Voice: teaching, precise, generous | Educators sound different from companies |

## Voice

**Confident, precise, generous. A senior practitioner explaining something properly to someone
who is genuinely trying to learn.**

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Explain the term the first time it appears | Assume the reader knows what an EIR is |
| Say "I have seen this go wrong" | Say "studies show" without a citation |
| Name what you don't know | Bluff past uncertainty |
| Say when the industry disagrees | Pick a side and present it as settled |
| Use short sentences | Write like a standard |
| Give the reader something to do today | End with "thoughts?" |

**Preferred words:** coordinated · structured · reliable · deliberate · buildability · handover ·
information · precision · traceable · auditable · discipline · rework

**Prohibited words:** game-changer · revolutionary · cutting-edge · ninja · guru · hack (as a
noun for expertise) · "the future of construction" · unlock · leverage (as a verb) · synergy

## Colour

| Role | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary — Deep Green** | `#1C4636` | Dark backgrounds, headline text on cream, rules |
| **Surface — Cream** | `#F5EFE8` | Light backgrounds, body text on green |
| **Accent — Clay** | `#B85C38` | Eyebrows, key numbers, one highlight per slide. **Max 10% of any layout.** |
| **Ink** | `#171717` | Body text on cream where green is too soft |
| **Muted** | `#6B6257` | Captions, footnotes, source lines |
| **Hairline** | `#D9CEC1` | Dividers and table rules on cream |

**Never use:** purple, blue, pink, gradients, drop shadows, glows, or neon.

**Two permitted schemes, alternating within a carousel:**

- **Cream scheme** — `#F5EFE8` background, `#1C4636` headline, `#171717` body, `#B85C38` accent
- **Dark scheme** — `#1C4636` background, `#F5EFE8` headline and body, `#B85C38` accent

**Rule:** slide 1 and the final CTA slide are always **dark**. Content slides are **cream**.
This gives every carousel a visible opening and closing without any extra design effort.

## Typography

| Level | Typeface | Size (1080×1350) | Weight | Case | Tracking |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Eyebrow** | Inter | 24 px | 600 | UPPERCASE | +8% |
| **Slide headline** | Bebas Neue | 96–120 px | Regular | ALL-CAPS | +2% |
| **Sub-headline** | Inter | 36 px | 600 | Sentence case | 0 |
| **Body** | Inter | 30 px | 400 | Sentence case | 0 |
| **List item** | Inter | 30 px | 400 | Sentence case | 0 |
| **Callout / number** | Bebas Neue | 140 px | Regular | — | 0 |
| **Caption / source** | Inter | 20 px | 400 | Sentence case | 0 |
| **Footer** | Inter | 20 px | 500 | Sentence case | +4% |

**Line height:** headlines 0.92 · sub-headlines 1.3 · body 1.5 · captions 1.4

**Hierarchy rule:** exactly **one** element per slide may use the headline size. If two things
compete for the eye, the slide has two ideas and should be two slides.

**Substitutions if Bebas Neue is unavailable:** none. Do not substitute. Bebas Neue is free on
Google Fonts and available in Canva.

## Layout grid and spacing

**For 1080 × 1350:**

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| **Outer margin** | 80 px on all sides |
| **Safe content area** | 920 × 1190 px |
| **Baseline grid** | 8 px |
| **Columns** | 6, gutter 24 px |
| **Space between eyebrow and headline** | 32 px |
| **Space between headline and body** | 48 px |
| **Space between list items** | 24 px |
| **Footer band height** | 96 px, bottom-aligned inside the margin |

**For 1080 × 1920 (Story / Status / TikTok):**

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| **Outer margin** | 80 px sides |
| **Top safe zone** | 250 px — clear of platform UI |
| **Bottom safe zone** | 320 px — clear of caption, CTA, and reply bar |
| **Actual content band** | 920 × 1350 px, vertically centred |

**Whitespace rule:** if a slide looks tight, remove a sentence. Never reduce the margin.

## Standard slide anatomy

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐  ← 80px margin
│                                      │
│  EYEBROW · CLAY · UPPERCASE INTER    │
│                                      │
│  BEBAS NEUE HEADLINE                 │
│  IN ALL CAPS                         │
│                                      │
│  ──────  clay rule, 4px × 120px      │
│                                      │
│  Inter body copy in sentence case,   │
│  set at 30px with 1.5 line height.   │
│  Maximum 45 words per slide.         │
│                                      │
│  [ diagram / icon / screenshot ]     │
│                                      │
│                                      │
│  ──────────────────────────  hairline│
│  Vollmann Akarakiri · BIM & Revit  3 │  ← footer + slide number
└──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**The clay rule (4 px × 120 px horizontal bar) appears on every slide.** It is the single most
recognisable element of the system — the thing that makes a slide identifiable as yours at
thumbnail size.

## Slide-count and word-count limits

| Platform | Slides | Words per slide | Hard limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| **LinkedIn carousel** | 8–12 | 35–45 | 55 |
| **Instagram carousel** | 6–8 | 15–25 | 30 |
| **Story / Status** | 1–5 | 12–20 | 25 |

**Rule:** if a slide exceeds its limit, it is two slides. This is not negotiable — carousels
fail because slides are dense, never because they are too many.

green). Size 64 × 64 px inline, 120 × 120 px as a slide feature.

**Sources:** Lucide, Phosphor (Regular weight), or Material Symbols Outlined. Pick one library
per carousel and never mix.

## Diagrams and illustration

**Preferred, in order:**

1. **Flow diagrams** — boxes with 2 px clay strokes, cream fill, Inter 24 px labels, arrows in
   clay. The BIM workflow diagram is the signature asset of this brand.
2. **Comparison tables** — two columns, hairline rules, clay used only in the header row.
3. **Annotated screenshots** — real Revit or Navisworks captures with clay callout numbers in
   40 px circles, and a caption naming the version.
4. **Layered axonometric** — exploded model diagrams for concepts like federation.

**Never:** stock photography, AI-generated photography, 3D-rendered generic "tech" imagery,
isometric people illustrations, or clip art.

**Screenshot rules:**

- Always state the Revit version in the caption.
- Crop to the relevant area. Never post a full-screen capture at carousel scale.
- Blank or blur any client-identifying project information.
- Set the Revit interface to a light theme so captures sit correctly on cream slides.

## Accessibility

| Requirement | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Contrast** | Cream on green and green on cream both exceed 4.5:1. Never place clay text on green — clay is for rules, numbers, and eyebrows on cream only. |
| **Minimum text size** | 24 px on a 1080 canvas. Nothing smaller, ever. |
| **Alt text** | Every LinkedIn and Instagram image gets alt text describing the content, not the design. |
| **Colour is never the only signal** | Any distinction shown in colour is also shown in text, position, or icon. |

## Canva build instructions

1. **Create a Brand Kit** — add the six hex codes above, and upload/enable Bebas Neue and Inter.
2. **Create two master pages** at 1080 × 1350: `SLIDE-DARK` and `SLIDE-CREAM`.
3. **Build the footer and clay rule once** on each master and lock those layers.
4. **Save as a Brand Template.** Every carousel starts by duplicating it.
5. **Build five text styles** matching the type scale table: Eyebrow, Headline, Sub, Body, Caption.
6. **Export carousels as PDF Standard**, not PNG — LinkedIn document posts require PDF.

## Figma build instructions

1. **Create local variables** for the six colours; name them `primary`, `surface`, `accent`,
   `ink`, `muted`, `hairline`.
2. **Create text styles** matching the type scale table exactly.
3. **Build two component frames** — `Slide/Dark` and `Slide/Cream` — at 1080 × 1350, with the
   footer, clay rule, and slide number as component properties.
4. **Set the 8 px baseline grid and 6-column layout grid** on both frames.
5. **Use Auto Layout** on the content stack with the spacing values from the layout table so
   slides reflow correctly when copy changes.
6. **Export** via `Export frames to PDF` for LinkedIn, PNG at 2× for Instagram.

## Pre-publication checklist

Run before every post. If any box fails, do not post.

- [ ] Zero emoji anywhere
- [ ] Headline in Bebas Neue ALL-CAPS, body in Inter sentence case
- [ ] Clay under 10% of the layout
- [ ] No purple, blue, or pink
- [ ] One headline-size element per slide
- [ ] Under the word limit on every slide
- [ ] Clay rule and footer present on every content slide
- [ ] Every technical claim traceable to `research/00-sources-register.md`
- [ ] No uncited statistic of any kind
- [ ] `[OPINION]` content is visibly framed as opinion
- [ ] Any `[CONTESTED]` topic is presented as contested
- [ ] Caption opens with a hook, not "I am"
- [ ] Caption ends with a CTA
- [ ] Hashtags after two line breaks, correct count for the platform
- [ ] Alt text written
- [ ] Screenshots cropped, version stated, client information removed
