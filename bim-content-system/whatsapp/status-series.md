# WhatsApp Status Series

WhatsApp Status is the highest-intent channel in this system. The people who view it already
know Vollmann — colleagues, former clients, contractors, classmates. It converts to enquiries
at a rate no public platform matches.

**Canvas:** 1080 × 1920 · top safe zone 250 px · bottom safe zone 320 px · content band
920 × 1350 centred. Full spec in `../BRAND.md`.

**Tone:** direct. Shorter than LinkedIn, warmer than Instagram, no performance. These people
know you.

**Cadence:** 3–4 status posts per week, matching the week's teaching topic. Status expires in
24 hours, so repetition across weeks is fine and actually helps.

---

## The five formats

| Format | Frames | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **1-image tip** | 1 | One idea, screenshot-able, no context needed |
| **Quick fact** | 1 | A surprising, citable fact |
| **Myth vs Fact** | 2 | Correction. Highest reply rate of any format. |
| **Mini tutorial** | 3–5 | A complete small workflow |
| **30-second series** | 5 | A numbered daily arc across one week |

## Format 1 — One-image tip

**Layout:** dark scheme · eyebrow top · Bebas headline · clay rule · 2–3 lines Inter 40 px ·
footer lockup bottom.

### Ready to build — 12 tips

| # | Eyebrow | Headline | Body |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | REVIT TIP | `IT IS NOT DELETED` | Something missing from a plan is almost always view range. Check that before you panic. |
| 2 | REVIT TIP | `LEVELS ARE NOT MADE IN PLAN` | You create levels in an elevation or section view. Every beginner learns this exactly once. |
| 3 | REVIT TIP | `LINK. NEVER IMPORT.` | An imported DWG is a frozen copy. An exploded one damages your project permanently. |
| 4 | REVIT TIP | `TAG PLUS SCHEDULE MEANS SHARED` | If a value must appear in both a tag and a schedule, it has to be a shared parameter. |
| 5 | REVIT TIP | `CLOSE THE VIEWS YOU ARE NOT USING` | Revit regenerates every open view. This is the cheapest performance fix there is. |
| 6 | BIM TIP | `SPECIFY INFORMATION, NOT DIMENSIONS` | "Provide asset data per the AIR" beats "provide 6D BIM" every time. |
| 7 | BIM TIP | `LINK, DO NOT MERGE` | Federation keeps authorship with the author. That is a liability decision, not just a technical one. |
| 8 | BIM TIP | `CLASHES CLOSED, NOT CLASHES FOUND` | A report of 8,000 clashes is a setup failure. The number that matters is how many were resolved. |
| 9 | REVIT TIP | `THE LOCATION LINE` | Walls that look right but measure wrong are almost always a location line problem. |
| 10 | BIM TIP | `NAME IT PROPERLY NOW` | Naming conventions are the most boring, highest-leverage thing in BIM. |
| 11 | REVIT TIP | `RELINQUISH BEFORE YOU LEAVE` | Borrowed elements block your colleagues all night. Relinquish all, every time. |
| 12 | BIM TIP | `IF YOU CANNOT CITE IT, DO NOT QUOTE IT` | Especially BIM ROI percentages. Someone senior will always ask where it came from. |

## Format 3 — Myth vs Fact (2 frames)

**Frame 1 — dark scheme.** Eyebrow "MYTH". The myth in Bebas, large. Nothing else. Deliberately
leaves the viewer sitting with the wrong idea for a moment.

**Frame 2 — cream scheme.** Eyebrow "FACT". The correction in Bebas. Two lines of Inter
explanation. Source line.

### Ready to build — 10 pairs

| # | Frame 1 — MYTH | Frame 2 — FACT | Explanation |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `BIM MEANS 3D` | `BIM MEANS MANAGED INFORMATION` | ISO 19650 is a standard about information management across the whole asset life. |
| 2 | `REVIT IS BIM` | `REVIT IS AN AUTHORING TOOL` | BIM is the process. Revit is where information is created — step three of nine. |
| 3 | `A SHARED FOLDER IS A CDE` | `A CDE HAS STATES` | Work in progress, shared, published, archived — with approvals between each. |
| 4 | `HIGHER LOD IS BETTER` | `THE RIGHT LOD IS THE ONE THE DECISION NEEDS` | Over-modelling is a real cost. EN 17412-1 exists because of it. |
| 5 | `THE MODEL GIVES YOU THE BOQ` | `THE MODEL GIVES YOU QUANTITIES` | Rates, waste, labour, plant and prelims are not in the model. |
| 6 | `A NICE RENDER IS A DIGITAL TWIN` | `A TWIN RECEIVES LIVE DATA` | The connection to the physical asset is what makes it a twin. |
| 7 | `7D MEANS SUSTAINABILITY` | `THE INDUSTRY DOES NOT AGREE` | Some say 6D. Some say 7D. Both positions are published. |
| 8 | `EIR MEANS EMPLOYER'S` | `EIR MEANS EXCHANGE` | It changed with ISO 19650. Using the old term dates you instantly. |
| 9 | `COBIE IS A SPREADSHEET` | `COBIE IS A SPECIFICATION` | Formally a subset of IFC. The spreadsheet is one carrier. |
| 10 | `WARNINGS DO NOT MATTER` | `SOME DOUBLE YOUR QUANTITIES` | Duplicate instances corrupt schedules silently. Triage weekly. |

## Format 4 — Mini tutorial (3–5 frames)

A complete workflow, small enough to finish in one sitting.

### Mini tutorial A — Find the element that disappeared (5 frames)

| Frame | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Dark.** `IT IS PROBABLY NOT DELETED` · Five things to check, in order. |
| 2 | **Cream.** `01 · VIEW RANGE` · View properties → View Range. Is the cut plane above or below your element? Screenshot of the dialog with the cut plane field circled in clay. |
| 3 | **Cream.** `02 · TEMPORARY HIDE` · A cyan border around the view means something is hidden or isolated. Type HR to reset. Screenshot of the cyan border. |
| 4 | **Cream.** `03 · VISIBILITY GRAPHICS` · Type VG. Check the category, then check the Filters tab. Screenshot. |
| 5 | **Dark.** `04 WORKSET · 05 PHASE` · Then design options. In that order, every time. Save this for the next time it happens. |

### Mini tutorial B — Set up a schedule that maintains itself (4 frames)

| Frame | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Dark.** `A SCHEDULE IS A VIEW, NOT A SPREADSHEET` |
| 2 | **Cream.** `01 · CREATE` · View tab → Schedules → Schedule/Quantities. Pick the category. |
| 3 | **Cream.** `02 · FIELDS` · Add only what the schedule has to answer. If you cannot say why a field is there, remove it. |
| 4 | **Dark.** `03 · IT IS BIDIRECTIONAL` · Edit a cell and you edit the element. If you are typing information the model should know, it belongs in the model. |

### Mini tutorial C — Weekly model health check (4 frames)

| Frame | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Dark.** `NINE CHECKS, EVERY FRIDAY` |
| 2 | **Cream.** `01–03` · Warnings count and type · file size trend · purge unused. |
| 3 | **Cream.** `04–06` · Audit on open · imported CAD instances · in-place family count. |
| 4 | **Dark.** `07–09` · Unplaced rooms · nested groups · views not on sheets. Track the trend, not the number. |

### Mini tutorial D — Link a CAD file without damaging your project (3 frames)

| Frame | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Dark.** `NEVER EXPLODE AN IMPORT` |
| 2 | **Cream.** `01 · LINK, DO NOT IMPORT` · Insert tab → Link CAD. Set positioning and units deliberately. Link into one dedicated view. |
| 3 | **Dark.** `02 · SWITCH IT OFF` · Once you have modelled from it, turn the link off. Exploding injects thousands of foreign line styles permanently. |

## Format 5 — 30-second educational series

Five frames released across one week, one per day, numbered so viewers know there is an arc.

### Series 1 — "What BIM is not" (Week 2)

| Day | Frame |
|---|---|
| Mon | `01 / 05` — BIM is not software. You can own every Autodesk licence and not be doing BIM. |
| Tue | `02 / 05` — BIM is not 3D. A model with no structured information is just a model. |
| Wed | `03 / 05` — BIM is not a deliverable. "Provide BIM" is not a specification. |
| Thu | `04 / 05` — BIM is not automatic. Placing a wall does not produce coordinated information. |
| Fri | `05 / 05` — BIM is not only for big projects. Small projects have less margin to lose. |

### Series 2 — "The nine steps" (Week 11)

| Day | Frame |
|---|---|
| Mon | `01 / 05` — Requirements. The client states what information they need. Usually skipped. |
| Tue | `02 / 05` — Planning. The BEP: roles, formats, coordinates, naming, milestones. |
| Wed | `03 / 05` — Authoring, federation, coordination. The part everyone thinks BIM is. |
| Thu | `04 / 05` — Documentation and construction. Drawings out of the model, not alongside it. |
| Fri | `05 / 05` — Handover and operation. Where the whole-life value is, and where it gets dropped. |

### Series 3 — "Five worksharing rules" (Week 30)

| Day | Frame |
|---|---|
| Mon | `01 / 05` — Never open the central model. Always create a local. |
| Tue | `02 / 05` — Synchronise every 30 to 60 minutes. Long gaps mean painful reconciliation. |
| Wed | `03 / 05` — Relinquish all before you leave. Borrowed elements block colleagues overnight. |
| Thu | `04 / 05` — Stagger your syncs. Everyone syncing at 5pm is a queue. |
| Fri | `05 / 05` — Worksets manage ownership and loading, not graphics. Use view templates for appearance. |

## Status CTA rules

WhatsApp Status has no link and no comment section. The only conversions are replies and DMs.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| End a series with "Reply if you want the full version" | Add a URL nobody can tap |
| Post the mini tutorials — they get replied to most | Post the same thing you posted on LinkedIn verbatim |
| Occasionally post work in progress with no lesson attached | Make every status educational — it starts reading as marketing |
| Reply to every single reply, personally | Broadcast |

`[OPINION]` Roughly one in six statuses should be ordinary — a site photo, a model you are
pleased with, a genuinely hard problem you are stuck on. Status is a relationship channel.
Perfect consistency here reads as advertising, and advertising gets muted.
