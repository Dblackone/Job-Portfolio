# Sources Register

Every source consulted during Phase 1 research, with its authority tier and what it is used to support.

**Research date:** July 2026

---

## How to read the authority tiers

| Tier | Meaning | How claims from it may be used |
|---|---|---|
| **A — Standards body / primary** | ISO, buildingSMART International, NIBS, CEN, UK BIM Framework, BSI | May be stated as definitive. Cite the standard number. |
| **B — Software vendor (primary for its own product)** | Autodesk official docs, Autodesk Platform Services, Dynamo project docs | Definitive **about that product only**. Never for neutral industry definitions. |
| **C — Recognised industry reference** | NBS, BIMForum, WBDG, Centre for Digital Built Britain, Designing Buildings | May be stated as industry consensus. Attribute by name. |
| **D — Peer-reviewed / academic** | Journals, university publications | May be stated with author and year. |
| **E — Secondary / encyclopaedic** | Wikipedia, vendor blogs, trade press | **Background only.** Never the sole support for a technical claim. Flag in the verification log. |

## Deliberately excluded

Search engine results returned a number of vendor marketing blogs and SEO content farms
(reseller blogs, "top 10 BIM benefits" listicles, AI-generated summaries). **None of these
were used.** Where a claim appeared only in that class of source, it was either dropped or
flagged as unverified in `01-verification-log.md`.
