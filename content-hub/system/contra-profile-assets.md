## Profile positioning

### Profile headline

**BIM, CAD, and Construction Documentation Support**

### Profile overview

I help architecture and construction teams turn project information into clear,
usable documentation and delivery-support systems.

My work covers Revit model and drawing-package review, 2D CAD documentation,
design-to-site coordination, progress reporting, procurement tracking, and
document control. I have worked across multiple companies and freelance
assignments, and I am building a focused independent practice around precise
information and practical construction support.

Every assignment begins with a defined scope: project stage, available files,
required output, deadline, assumptions, and review boundaries.

### Services to publish

1. **Revit BIM model and drawing review**
   - Review an agreed package for coordination, documentation, and information-quality issues.
   - Deliver a structured issue summary and review assumptions.

2. **CAD documentation and drawing updates**
   - Update supplied drawings from markups, dimensions, sketches, or reference files.
   - Deliver agreed sheets in the required editable and PDF formats.

3. **Construction reporting and procurement tracking**
   - Organize supplied project information into a practical tracker and reporting structure.
   - Support progress updates, actions, procurement packages, and document control.
