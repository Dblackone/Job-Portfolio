# Warm referral request kit

This kit supports referrals from former collaborators, past clients, classmates,
and trusted professional contacts. It is separate from the paused direct-company
outreach campaign. These messages are drafts for manual personalization and
sending; no referral request has been sent by this file.

## Position to lead with

I am currently taking on focused freelance support in:

- CAD drafting and technical documentation
- Revit/BIM model and drawing support
- Construction reporting, document control, and procurement tracking
- Scoped takeoff and measurement support, without presenting this as licensed
  quantity surveying

Use one service lane per message. Do not send a general list of every capability.

## Personalization and follow-up rules

1. Choose five people with a real prior connection; do not send this as a cold
   batch.
2. Replace the opening with a specific shared project, role, or conversation.
3. Ask for one introduction, not a general broadcast.
4. Send one follow-up after five to seven days if there is no response.
5. Record recipient, date sent, service lane, response, next action, and any
   resulting project in the acquisition tracker.
6. Count a referral as a lead only after the introduced person expresses an
   identifiable need and agrees to a next step.
