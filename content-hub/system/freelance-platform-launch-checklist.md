# Freelance Platform Launch Checklist

**Selected platforms:** Upwork and Contra  
**Purpose:** Test near-term freelance income while the company portfolio and referral network continue to grow.

Record the baseline and each review in `freelance-platform-test-log.csv`.
Use `freelance-pricing-framework.md` to qualify budgets before applying or accepting work.

## Upwork launch

- [ ] Create or update the profile using `freelance-profile-assets.md`.
- [ ] Use the title: `BIM and CAD Documentation Specialist | Revit Review and Construction Support`.
- [ ] Add the three proof links from the profile asset file.
- [ ] Select skills that can be evidenced: CAD documentation, Revit/BIM review,
  construction reporting, procurement tracking, and document control.
- [ ] Complete identity, payment, and availability settings before applying.
- [ ] Apply only to jobs with a clear scope and a close match to an available proof sample.
- [ ] Begin with a maximum of five targeted applications per week.
- [ ] Record every application in `freelance-opportunities.csv`.

## Contra launch

- [ ] Create a free profile using `contra-profile-assets.md`.
- [ ] Add the portfolio, BIM portfolio, and BIM review project links.
- [ ] Publish three service offers: BIM review, CAD documentation, and construction
  reporting/procurement tracking.
- [ ] Add a referral/contact route for people who already know the work.
- [ ] Confirm available Nigerian payout methods before treating Contra as a payment route.
- [ ] Do not subscribe to Contra Pro until the free profile produces measurable interest.
- [ ] Record platform-sourced enquiries and referral enquiries separately.

## 30-day test rules

- Week 1: complete profiles and publish proof-backed services.
- Weeks 2–4: submit up to five matched Upwork applications per week and respond to
  every qualified Contra enquiry.
- No broad, generic applications and no unsupported credentials.
- No new direct-partnership batch during this test.
- Create content only when it supports a live offer, answers a buyer objection, or
  documents a verified project lesson.

## Measurement

Track separately for each platform:

- profile views or impressions;
- applications or proposals sent;
- replies and qualified conversations;
- consultations or interviews;
- proposals issued;
- work won;
- income received in ₦;
- repeat or recurring work.

An application is not a lead until the client replies or expresses identifiable
interest. Proposed, won, and received amounts must remain separate.

## Decision after 30 days

- Keep the channel that produces qualified conversations or paid work.
- Rewrite the channel that produces attention without relevant enquiries.
- Consider adding Guru only if both selected platforms produce insufficient
  opportunity and the account/payment setup is practical.
