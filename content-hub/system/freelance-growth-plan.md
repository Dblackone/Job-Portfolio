# Freelance Growth Plan

**Current phase:** Build personal income while the company grows.  
**Primary channels:** Freelance marketplaces, referrals, paid knowledge, and recurring support.  
**Deferred channel:** New direct partnership outreach.

## Business context

The founder is early in building his own company, with approximately four to
five major company projects and a wider history of freelance work across several
companies. Near-term work must be easy for a new client to understand, small
enough to buy with limited trust, and deliverable without implying a scale or
credential that has not been verified.

The five direct-company messages already sent remain a monitored exception. Do
not start another direct-partnership batch until the marketplace and referral
system has been tested.

## Service lanes

### 1. CAD and technical documentation

- 2D drafting and drawing updates
- drawing-set cleanup and sheet consistency checks
- architectural documentation support
- detail and annotation production from supplied information

### 2. BIM and Revit support

- model and drawing package review
- Revit documentation support
- coordination issue registers
- design-to-site information checks
- model organization and deliverable preparation

### 3. Construction administration and project controls

- progress-reporting templates
- programme and action tracking
- procurement logs and package tracking
- meeting minutes, decision registers, and document control
- construction delivery reporting support

### 4. Takeoff and estimating support

Offer quantity takeoff, measurement, and estimating assistance only within the
actual project scope and competence. Do not present this as licensed quantity
surveying, professional certification, valuation, or statutory advice. Where a
qualified quantity surveyor is required, work under the appropriate professional
review or refer the client onward.

### 5. Procore and construction software support

Treat the current Procore course as a capability-building track. First offer
administrative support, setup assistance, document workflows, and reporting
support that can be demonstrated. Do not claim Procore certification or advanced
implementation experience until independently verified.

## Channel strategy

| Channel | Role now | First test |
|---|---|---|
| Upwork | Find live, defined BIM, CAD, documentation, and construction-support jobs | Apply only to tightly matched jobs with a relevant proof sample |
| Fiverr | Sell fixed-scope, productized services | Publish three clear gigs with deliverables, assumptions, and boundaries |
| Referrals | Convert existing relationships into warm introductions | Ask past collaborators and contacts for one specific introduction |
| Paid knowledge | Monetize practical reviews, templates, and focused guidance | Offer a defined paid review or working session |
| Recurring support | Turn one-off work into monthly reporting or BIM support | Offer a small recurring scope after a successful first assignment |
| Direct partnerships | Deferred for now | Monitor the five already-contacted companies; no new batch |

## Offer ladder

1. **Low-friction entry:** a defined drawing, model, document, or reporting review.
2. **Project assignment:** documentation, BIM coordination, procurement support, or
   construction reporting.
3. **Recurring support:** a monthly coordination, reporting, or document-control
   arrangement.
4. **Knowledge products:** templates, training, or practical implementation
   sessions based on demonstrated experience.

Do not build a passive product before the underlying service has produced repeated
questions or paid demand. Productized services are the nearer-term side-income
path; genuinely passive income is a later experiment.

## 30-day operating loop

### Week 1 — Positioning

- Build one focused Upwork profile around BIM, CAD, and construction support.
- Define three Fiverr services from the service lanes above.
- Select one proof sample per service from verified work.
- Write a short capability statement that distinguishes company projects from
  personal freelance work.

### Week 2 — Marketplace launch

- Publish the three Fiverr listings.
- Submit a small number of highly matched Upwork proposals.
- Record every application in `freelance-opportunities.csv`.
- Do not apply to work that requires an unverified licence, certification, or
  software capability.

### Week 3 — Referrals and paid knowledge

- Contact warm professional connections with a specific referral request.
- Offer one defined paid review or working session.
- Turn repeated questions into a reusable checklist or template only after they
  show genuine demand.

### Week 4 — Conversion review

- Count applications, profile views where available, replies, interviews,
  consultations, proposals, wins, and received payments separately.
- Keep the service lane that generates qualified conversations.
- Rewrite or retire listings that generate attention but no relevant enquiries.
- Decide whether a successful one-off service can become recurring support.

## Content rule

Content is supporting evidence, not the primary activity. Create a post only when
it does at least one of the following:

- proves a service lane;
- answers a buyer objection;
- points to a live marketplace listing or referral offer; or
- documents a verified project lesson that improves trust.

If none of these conditions is present, work on applications, delivery proof, or
follow-up instead of generating another post.

## Measurement rules

- Applications are not leads until a client replies or expresses identifiable
  interest.
- A reply becomes a lead only when it has a real contact, source, service lane,
  next action, and due date.
- Proposed value, won value, and received income remain separate.
- Never claim a certification, licence, project result, or software proficiency
  that has not been verified.
