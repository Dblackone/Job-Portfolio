## Priority targets

When the Upwork profile is ready, prioritize listings that ask for:

1. Revit-to-CAD conversion or drawing updates from supplied files.
2. Architectural drafting and construction-documentation production.
3. BIM documentation review, issue tracking, or model organization.
4. Progress reporting, document control, or construction coordination support.

Use one relevant proof link and one tailored proposal from
`upwork-proposal-templates.md` for each application.

## Do not target yet

Do not apply to listings that require any of the following unless the capability
is independently verified:

- advanced Navisworks clash detection or federated-model management;
- scan-to-BIM from point clouds;
- MEP modelling or specialist engineering design;
- licensed architectural, engineering, or quantity-surveying sign-off;
- Procore certification or advanced implementation;
- expert-level experience that cannot be supported by the portfolio.

## Qualification rules

Before recording an opportunity in `freelance-opportunities.csv`, confirm:

- the listing is still open;
- the client’s scope matches an available service lane;
- the required software and experience are verified;
- the expected output can be delivered from the supplied information;
- the listing URL and next-action date are recorded.

Only a client reply or identifiable interest should promote an opportunity into
the lead tracker.
