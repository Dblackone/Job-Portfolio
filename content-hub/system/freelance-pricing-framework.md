# Freelance Pricing and Qualification Framework

**Purpose:** Protect limited time, avoid underpriced work, and convert freelance
assignments into sustainable income while the company grows.

## Qualification gates

Accept or apply only when all four conditions are true:

1. The requested output matches a verified service lane.
2. The required software, standards, and delivery capacity are available.
3. The client can provide the files, dimensions, markups, or workflow inputs needed.
4. The likely value justifies the time, revisions, communication, and platform fees.

If any condition is unknown, ask a scope question before quoting.

## Quote method

Estimate the assignment using:

`production time + review time + communication/admin time + revision allowance`

Then confirm:

- exact deliverables;
- file formats and software versions;
- number of included revision rounds;
- deadline and response expectations;
- client-supplied information and assumptions; and
- payment milestones for larger assignments.

Never quote from the headline budget alone when the deliverables are undefined.

## Offer ladder

### Entry offer

Use a small, paid review or defined drawing update when trust is limited. The
scope must be narrow enough to deliver without unpaid discovery work.

### Project offer

Use a fixed scope for a drawing package, BIM review, construction tracker, or
reporting system. Break larger work into milestones tied to reviewable outputs.

### Recurring offer

After a successful first assignment, offer a repeatable monthly scope such as
document control, progress reporting, procurement tracking, or coordination review.

## Reject or renegotiate when

- the budget is materially below the time required;
- the client requests unlimited revisions;
- the listing requires an unverified licence, code jurisdiction, or software;
- the client expects professional sign-off outside the agreed role; or
- the project depends on missing information that the client will not provide.

The $10 Revit-to-AutoCAD listing is the reference example: it matches the skill
lane but fails the income-protection test and should not set the normal price.

## Platform adjustment

For Upwork, account for the contract service fee and proposal effort before
accepting a budget. For Contra, account for payment-processing or platform fees
and the time required to bring or qualify a referral. Do not pay for premium
platform features until the free profile produces measurable interest.

## Income record

Record proposed value, won value, and received income separately. A high quoted
amount is not income until payment is actually received.
