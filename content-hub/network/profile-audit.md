### Primary diagnosis

The profile is credible but too broad for search and conversion. The headline and
About section mix senior construction leadership, design-build delivery, BIM/CAD,
and an emerging data-analyst identity. A visitor may understand the background but
still not know the first service or role to contact you about.

The highest-leverage change is to lead with the service lane that is already backed
by the portfolio: BIM and construction documentation support. Keep leadership and
design-build experience as proof, not as four competing headlines.
